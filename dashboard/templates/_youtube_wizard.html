YouTube

Set up YouTube playback

Right now /watch won't play videos. YouTube refuses to serve stream URLs to fetchproxies' source IPs (datacenter / VPN / residential proxy) — it returns "Sign in to confirm you're not a bot" instead. Pick one of the options below to authenticate companion as a real Google account; from then on the source IP no longer matters and you can drop the proxy chain.

Sign in with Google recommended

One-time TV-style device authorization. Takes about a minute on your phone.

  • Refresh token stored on disk; survives reboots and redeploys.
  • Companion only requests YouTube playback scope — Google won't see anything beyond that.
  • You can use a throwaway Google account if you'd rather not link your primary one.

Paste browser cookies

Fast if you're already signed into youtube.com here.

  • Cookies expire faster than OAuth — re-paste every few weeks.
  • Get invalidated when you sign out of YouTube anywhere or hit a Google security event.
  • Use this if Google won't issue OAuth for your account region.
Show paste form

DevTools → Application → Cookies → youtube.com. The SID / HSID / SSID / SAPISID set is the important one.

Why is this needed?

YouTube ties video stream URLs to a "Proof of Origin" (PO) token. Generating a valid PO from a headless environment used to work; YouTube has tightened bot detection and now datacenter / VPN / residential-proxy IPs (which is everything fetchproxies has — Mullvad, Bright Data) get a stub response with no playable formats.

An authenticated session — Google OAuth refresh token, or full youtube.com cookies — sidesteps the PO requirement entirely. Once one is in place you can flip egress below to direct and stop routing through the proxy chain altogether.
